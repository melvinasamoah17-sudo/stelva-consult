How this works

Since the website doesn't have its own server/database, adding news happens in two quick steps:

  1. Fill in the form below and click "Add to list." Repeat for as many items as you like, or edit/delete existing ones.
  2. Click "Download updated news-data.json", then upload that one file to the website's GitHub repository (replacing the old one) — the same way images or files are usually uploaded there. The live site updates automatically within a minute or two.
